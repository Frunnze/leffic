Action potentials

The action potential is the electrical signal a neuron uses to carry information along its axon. It is all-or-nothing: once the membrane crosses threshold, the full spike follows.

This document covers the resting state, the rising and falling phases, the refractory period, and how myelination changes conduction velocity.
